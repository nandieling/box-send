    ‖ THANKS ‖ Huge thanks goes to @ohtrack who bought the USA Universal 1999 NTSC DVD (for hefty price) and made this remux possible with original 4-channel mix. Once again I am eternaly grateful!
    ‖ TOOLS ‖ DGDemux with "mergeDV" function to demux UHD DV HDR disc, eac3to for demuxing 1080p disc, SubMover for PGS delay, DTS-MAS for DTS audio reencode (more in the notes), ffsubsync for syncing SRT subs.
    ‖ VIDEO ‖ Only one source and that is restoration KL UHD release. Looks fabulous and adds extra detail over 1080p version. Fairly bright which was a surprise. L5 crop checked and verified. L1 scene changes also verified to be right in the final mkv.
    ‖ AUDIO ‖ New "5.1" 2026 remix made default even though it: has C channel void of sound (except dither), surround speakers are mastered too loud, has mastering error for some sound cues, "Simon Zealotes" mix is restored from subpar source. Still offers unmatched atmosperics that pushed it to first spot. 5.1 DTS-HD MA restoration remix stripped of one void channel (C) and DTS-MASed to 4.1 ch. layout. Original 4-channel mix added from NTSC DVD as a second option. Matrixed 2.0 offerings of original 4-channel mix from Blu-rays ommitted since 4-ch mix exists and was sourced (thanks @ohtrack!). Isolated score track without vocal sourced from FRA BD. Original commentary from Universal VC1 USA BD for best DR and surround tag, rest are new commentaries from KL USA restoration disc.
    • New "5.1" 2026 remix - New Kino Lorber restoration disc promised brand new 5.1 remix. Well, in classic KL way They delivered only about half of that. First of all BDInfo reports this track as 24-bit, but it is padded and dithered 16-bit track. The expectation was that this would be 5.1 remix with added surround activity, since there exist (maybe existed due to 2008 Universal storage fire) 4-channel 35mm and 6-channel 70mm original audio mix tapes for this movie. In this case the new 5.1 remix is in fact 4.1 remix. The center channel has only dithering and has no actual sound. Strange choice to say the lest when with 4-channel mix you have descrete L/C/R layout and one surround. The 2026 remix on itself is good and adds much more needed liveliness and balance to the track. It is not without its flaws however. Something you will hear almost instantly is that surrounds appear to be mixed too loud and sometimes completely overshadows what is happening on the screen in front of you. Bass also seemed too loud in some cases. Both of these can be adressed on the spot by lowering gain on your surrounds/LFE so it was not a huge problem. What is not addressable is mastering errors for certain sounds effects that are either almost completely missing or have such low volume that they are lacking. Great example is at about 00:39:00. In dance/song number "Simon Zealotes" there appears to be used subpar source for this mix, since audio drops in quality quite rapidly (but after that recovers). Even after all this I thought the new remix sounded very good and it deserves the first spot. The "5.1" track was demuxed with eac3too to wavs with -21ms delay, void center channel was ommited and remaining channels were encoded back to DTS-HD MA 4.1 via DTS-MAS. Playback then tested and verified.
    • Original 4-channel mix (DD 4.0) - All BDs (except the new KL disc) came with matrixed 2.0 audio track which (as far as I could tell) is derived from the original 4-channel mix. Some of these tracks have horrible normalization applied and almost no dynamics at all (except CEE/EUR discs). Mastering was also off since voices overpower the instruments by quite a bit. For this reason I was determined to source the original 4-ch mix and thanks to @ohtrack (big shoutout) I was able to include this original from USA Universal 1999 NTSC DVD. The separation is second to none and dynamics are great.The mix does not go as down as the lossless stereo matrixed BD counterparts. Other then that it is great mix and was a strong contender to have a default flag. I went for the new remix in the end. DD 4.0 audio had to be losslessly edited in eac3to to fit restoration video source, since USA NTSC DVD has few added frames. Remaining 11ms delay after cuts could not be removed, but the delay is small enouch that it could not be heard in the actual playback.
    • New 2026 stereo remix New stereo remix is just 5.1 (4.1) remix without surrounds and separate LFE channel. The presentation is much closer to the original and I found no excuse to also not include it since (like the "5.1" remix) this sounds amazing and is good option for those with no surround setup (since "5.1" remix has surrounds too loud the downmix done on AVRs would sound not well ballanced).
    • Isolated score and effects without vocals This track is only available on 2017 FRA disc and is 2.0 matrixed sound. Included for those, who want to sing along and for fullest remux possible :).
    ‖ COMMENTARY ‖ 3 commentaries in total. First commentary (with Norman Jewison and Ted Neeley) is older commentary and is on multiple discs. CEE/EUR/USA(VC-1) same encode with surround tag. FRA/USA(2026) worse dynamics and spectrogram. Went with USA VC-1 disc and delayed with eac3too. 2 new commentaries are same across 1080p and 2160p KL discs.
